About me

I’m a 19-year-old sophomore pursuing a BA in Multimedia Studies. I balance my time between academics and organizational activities. Outside of school, I enjoy photography and playing Valorant. I’m also part of the Publicity and Publications Committee of UP Optics, where I get to explore my passion for photography and media while contributing to the university community.

Things I love

  • Cameras
  • Taking pictures
  • Listening to music
